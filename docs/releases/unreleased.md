# Open Model Gateway: unreleased

Notes for changes on `main` after [v0.15.1](v0.15.1.md). They become the next release's notes.

## Contributors

- The supply-chain test now refuses Dockerfile constructs that could make it read a stage BuildKit does not build, and the two ways other than `RUN` to bring outside content into a stage past its checks of `FROM` lines, which are `ADD` and an external image in `COPY --from=` or a mount (#485). Refused: `<<` in any instruction (heredocs, also split across a `\` continuation, here-strings and shifts), parser-directive comments (`# escape=`, `# syntax=`) and `#!` lines, any instruction outside a fixed list (so `ADD`, `ONBUILD`, `MAINTAINER` and the `//syntax=` line that selects another build frontend), non-ASCII and control characters and CRLF, a `FROM` that spans lines or takes its image from an `ARG`, blank lines or comments inside a continued instruction, `COPY --from=` or a `RUN --mount=` `from=` that is anything but the name of an earlier stage (no stage index and no external image, pinned by digest or not), and `COPY`/`RUN` flags with characters outside a plain set (no variables, quotes, backslashes or globs). What a `RUN` command does is not checked: the builds still fetch from the network (`npm ci`, `cargo build`). Neither Dockerfile used any of the refused forms. Each failure names the file, the line and the rule; the rules are in [verification.md](../verification.md#supply-chain).
