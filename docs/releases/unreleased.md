# Open Model Gateway: unreleased

Notes for changes on `main` after [v0.13.0](v0.13.0.md). They become the next release's notes.

## New features

- **Access review campaigns** (#90, part 1). A Platform Admin starts a one-shot campaign (`POST /api/v1/platform/access-reviews`) that snapshots the live shared Team/Project memberships and/or Admin and Auditor platform role grants it covers, optionally narrowed to listed workspaces, due 1 to 90 days later (default 14), and closes it when done. Platform Admins and Auditors read campaigns and their items, each showing the person, role, source (manual, group or bootstrap) and whether the grant is still in effect. Browser session only. At most 20 open campaigns and 5,000 items per campaign. Requires migration **0076** and the updated runtime grants.
- **Access review decisions** (#90, part 2). Reviewers see the items they may decide in `GET /api/v1/me/access-reviews`: the owners and admins of each Team or Project review its memberships, Platform Admins review platform roles, nobody reviews their own access, and a Platform Admin can assign an item to one specific eligible reviewer. They decide **keep**, or **flag_idp** for group-provided access to be fixed at the identity provider; decisions are append-only and audited, and the latest counts. Requires migration **0077** and the updated runtime grants.
- **Removing access from a review** (#90, part 3). A reviewer's **remove** decision removes manual or bootstrap access at once, through the same paths and protections as removing it directly: a membership (owners only by an owner of the workspace or a Platform Admin; the last owner protected; the person's keys there revoked once no membership remains) or a platform role (only that grant; the last Platform Admin protected; a person left without any platform role loses platform access, as when revoking the role directly). A refused removal records nothing; group-provided access is flagged for the identity provider instead. Requires migration **0078**.
- **Access review reminders** (#90, part 4). Reviewers get an in-app notification for each open campaign in which they still have access to decide: when it opens, again within 24 hours of its due date, and again once overdue. It disappears once they have decided, the items are assigned to someone else, or the campaign closes. In-app only. Requires migration **0079**.
- **Access reviews in the dashboard** (#90, part 5). Admin › **Access reviews**: start a review, follow its decisions, assign reviewers and close it; Auditors read. **Review access** (Workspace sidebar): keep, remove or flag what you are asked to review, with a confirmation that says what a removal does. Reminders open it.
- **Access review export** (#90, part 6). `GET /api/v1/platform/access-reviews/{id}/export` (Platform Admins and Auditors, browser session only; **Export CSV** on a review's page) downloads every item with its latest decision, decider and reviewer as CSV (cells a spreadsheet would run as formulas are neutralized), audited `access_review.exported`. Each item now says whether anyone may review it (`reviewable`), and a review's page marks items with **No reviewer** for a Platform Admin to assign (#469).

## Behaviour changes

- `GET /api/v1/me/notifications` items can be access review reminders (`source: "access_review"`), and the summary's `unread` counts them; clients that assume every item is an alert incident or an access request need the new contract.
- Management API: nine new operations (**272**); new `409` reasons `access_review_limit`, `access_review_too_large`, `access_review_empty`, `access_review_changed`, `access_review_closed`, `access_review_item_inactive`, `access_review_flag_requires_group`, `access_review_reviewer_ineligible`, `access_review_own_access`, `access_review_remove_requires_manual`, `access_review_owner_removal` and `access_review_removal_refused`; new audit actions `access_review.created`, `access_review.closed`, `access_review.exported` (resource `access_review`), `access_review.decided` and `access_review.reviewer_assigned` (resource `access_review_item`).
